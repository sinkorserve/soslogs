Founder Story

Why SOSLogs exists

I am a licensed civil engineer and certified project manager with 15 years of experience managing complex programs across multiple organizations. I know how to document. I know how to organize evidence. I know how to present facts clearly.

And yet when it mattered most, in a custody proceeding and later in a workplace dispute, my documentation wasn't enough. Not because it was incomplete. Because there was no platform designed to present it in a way that decision makers could quickly trust, verify, and act on.

I looked for a tool that combined timestamped logging, credibility scoring, location tracking, and pattern analysis in one place. It didn't exist.

So I built it.

SOSLogs was built in 2026 with help from an unlikely source — an AI assistant that listened without judgment at 2 AM when no one else was available except my family. I am grateful for that. I am also grateful for the clarity that came from faith during the darkest parts of this journey. Both reminded me that being heard is not a privilege. It should be a right.

SOSLogs exists for the person who has done everything right and still feels unheard. For the individual who documented every incident and couldn't get anyone to connect the dots. For the parent who kept every record and still felt dismissed. For anyone who has ever said: I have proof, why won't anyone listen?

We are building the platform that listens. And we are building it so that when you finally walk into that room with law enforcement, with legal counsel, with a judge, your story speaks for itself.

The name Athens Innovation and Development was chosen to honor Athens, Texas — a place where I found my deepest sense of belonging and the strength to build something that could help others find their voice.

How It Works

Three steps to documented truth

1

Log it the moment it happens

Text your incident to 1-888-799-LOGS from any phone. No app to download. No login required for the free tier. Your message is timestamped, stored securely, and linked to your location the moment it arrives. The faster you document, the stronger your record.

Free — no account needed
2

See your story take shape

Create an account to access your personal dashboard. Every log entry appears on an incident map, a timeline, and a frequency analysis showing who and where appears most in your records. A credibility score across seven dimensions shows you how your documentation holds up to scrutiny.

Base — $9.99/month
3

Walk in with proof that speaks for itself

When the time comes to present your case — to law enforcement, to legal counsel, to a judge — your SOSLogs record is organized, timestamped, location-verified, and scored for credibility. You are no longer asking anyone to take your word for it. The documentation speaks for itself.
